Control this page with your eyes

Able Cursor Gaze Mode uses your webcam to estimate where you look and moves a cursor on this page. Everything is processed on your device. No video is uploaded or stored. Tracking can be imperfect, and every person is different.

Before you start

  1. Sit comfortably in front of the computer.
  2. Keep your face at a comfortable distance from the webcam.
  3. Keep your eyes visible, with steady lighting in front of you.
  4. Follow the on-screen dots with your eyes (calibration).
  5. Learn how the gaze cursor moves.
  6. Choose your clicking method: dwell or double blink.
  7. Pause or stop any time: the Pause and Stop buttons, or press Escape.

Clicking I Agree does not turn the camera on. The browser will ask for camera permission separately, when you press Start Setup. This is a prototype, not a certified medical device.

Help

What is real

Live camera, face and iris tracking (MediaPipe, on your device), a calibration that trains a regression, a gaze cursor, dwell clicking, double-blink clicking, and a floating panel inside this page. I have not measured accuracy with a study, so treat it as an experiment.

Limits

  • The cursor works only on this page. It cannot control other apps.
  • Glasses, glare, dim light, and head movement lower accuracy. Recalibrate if the cursor drifts.
  • Natural blinking varies. If double blink triggers by mistake or not at all, use dwell.
  • The "Pop out" window floats above other apps but works with mouse and keyboard only, not gaze.
  • If you pause by gaze, resume with the mouse, the P key, or a caregiver.

Indian Standards (BIS)

Designed with reference to IS 17802 (Part 1):2021, Accessibility for the ICT Products and Services (BIS adoption of EN 301 549 v3.2.1), which the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Rules name for websites and applications. This is a self-assessment. It has not been audited or certified.

  • Works without a mouse, keyboard or touch once calibrated (limited manipulation or strength).
  • Large targets, high-contrast theme, three text sizes, visible focus.
  • Status is given in words, not colour alone, and announced to screen readers.
  • Explicit consent before the camera starts. Video stays on the device.
  • Mouse and keyboard remain available as alternatives.

Keys

Escape stops tracking at once. P pauses or resumes.

Privacy

Camera frames stay on your device. The tracker files download once from jsDelivr and Google. We save only your settings and a flag that you finished onboarding.
