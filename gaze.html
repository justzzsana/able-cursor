    Welcome to Able Cursor

    Able Cursor helps you control supported on-screen elements using your eye movements. Your webcam estimates where you are looking, and you can select items using dwell clicking or an optional double-blink gesture. This is an assistive prototype. Accuracy varies with your webcam, lighting, positioning and your own eye movements. Everything is processed on your device. No video is uploaded or stored.

    Before you start

    1. Sit comfortably in front of the computer.
    2. Keep your face at a comfortable distance from the webcam.
    3. Keep your eyes visible, with steady lighting in front of you.
    4. Follow the on-screen dots with your eyes (calibration).
    5. Learn how the gaze cursor moves.
    6. Choose your clicking method: dwell or double blink.
    7. Pause or stop any time: the Pause and Stop buttons, or press Escape.

    Clicking I Agree does not turn the camera on. The browser will ask for camera permission separately, when you press Get Started. This is a prototype, not a certified medical device.

    Choose how to click

    Dwell clicking (recommended)

    Look at a button. A ring fills around the cursor. When it is full, the button is pressed. Look away to cancel.

    Double blink (optional)

    Look at a button and blink twice on purpose. It may work differently for each person, so dwell is the fallback.

    Help

    Set up

    • Webcam: at eye level, lens clean.
    • Face: an arm's length away, both eyes in the green box, light in front of you.
    • Camera: press Start Camera and choose Allow.
    • Calibration: look at each dot until it moves. Keep still.

    Use it

    • Cursor: the ring follows where you look. Buttons light up when it is near.
    • Dwell: keep looking at a button until the ring fills.
    • Double blink: blink twice on purpose while looking at a button.
    • Pause: the Pause button, or press P. Stop camera: the Stop button, or press Escape.

    Hey Cursor

    On the Live demo page press Enable voice and allow the microphone. Say "Hey Cursor", then a command: start tracking, pause tracking, resume tracking, start calibration, turn on dwell clicking, turn on blink clicking, open help, stop camera. It works in Chrome and Edge while this page is open and visible. It stops when you disable it.

    If something goes wrong

    • Cursor is off: add light, keep both eyes visible, sit as you did in calibration, then Recalibrate. Resizing the window also needs a recalibration.
    • Clicking does not work: use a longer dwell time, or check that a button is highlighted.
    • Blink does not work: change blink sensitivity in Settings, or use dwell.
    • No camera: close Zoom or Teams, allow the camera in the address bar, press Start Camera.

    Limits and privacy

    This is a prototype, not a medical device, and accuracy varies. The cursor works only on this page. Camera frames stay on your device. We save only your settings and a flag that you finished onboarding. Chrome's speech recognition sends audio to Google while voice is on.
