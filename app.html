Able Cursor: the real tool

Real webcam gaze, real blink detection, real microphone. Camera frames are processed on your device and never stored or sent. Chrome's speech recognition sends audio to Google's servers.

Start with step 1.

1. Start camera and tracker

2. Calibrate (about 25 seconds, no clicking)

Sit still, look at each dot. It goes fullscreen so zones match your screen.

3. Start the microphone

Then say "Hey Cursor".

4. Optional: floating window (Chrome or Edge)

Dictation target

Commands: open Google (or YouTube, Wikipedia, Maps, Gmail, Translate, News, Weather, Music, Docs, Calendar), search something, type text, scan, yes, sleep. Keys: Space or Enter select while scanning, S scans, Escape cancels. Deliberate eye-close for about half a second also selects while scanning.

Ambient: asleep. Say Hey Cursor.
